# Structured Calendar Eligibility and Explicit Overlap Policy on Rental Offers (Extends ADR 0018)

Standalone `rental_offers` (`daily`, `package`, `hourly`, `monthly`; never `extension`) carry optional nullable `calendar_eligibility` (JSONB) and `overlap_policy` (TEXT) columns so duration-based offers and calendar-restricted offers (such as a standard 2-day package alongside a Saturday–Sunday weekend package) coexist verbatim on the same motorcycle without label-only hacks or flattening into daily rates. In v1, `calendar_eligibility` represents a shop-local Saturday–Sunday weekend window (`{ kind: 'weekend', startDay: 'saturday', endDay: 'sunday', startTime: null | 'HH:MM', endTime: null | 'HH:MM', timezoneBasis: 'shop_local' }`), preserving unspecified pickup/return hours as `null` rather than equating two days to 48 hours or fabricating hours; `overlap_policy` is strictly `null` on unrestricted offers and explicitly `'unknown' | 'alternative' | 'replaces_standard'` (defaulting to `'unknown'`) on calendar-restricted offers, and precedence is never inferred from price, specificity, or supplier labels. Calendar eligibility participates in duplicate-offer identity so identical-duration standard and weekend offers coexist, while calendar-restricted offers are excluded from legacy `rental_rate_tiers` / `motorcycle_rentals.rental_rate_per_day` projections and from duration-only price filters (`durationDays`, `minPrice`/`maxPrice`), and any legacy `rates[]`/scalar write against a motorcycle holding calendar-restricted offers fails with `409 Conflict`. Shop-local calendar matching is evaluated through pure deterministic helpers taking an explicit IANA `shopTimezone` parameter—avoiding premature timezone schema changes on `rental_shops`—and returns explicit unevaluable states whenever dates, timezone, or overlap precedence are missing.
